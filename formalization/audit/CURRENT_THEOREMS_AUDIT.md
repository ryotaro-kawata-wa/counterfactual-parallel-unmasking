## Commit-feedback correspondence issue (blocking unrestricted lower coverage)

The manuscript's operation-visible observation includes both the probability
vectors returned at a commit and its sampled values. `Program.commit` currently
passes only sampled values to the continuation. Thus the existing lower proofs
cover a restricted controller class; they cannot yet certify the unrestricted
manuscript lower theorem. The concrete upper sampler never needs that extra
feedback, so its construction and guarantees are unaffected.

The intended repair preserves the existing Program semantics but strengthens
the lower adversary model with free reads of current-history marginals. An
explicit observed-commit controller can then be simulated with unchanged paper
query/round budgets and exactly the same output law. On the matching hard family,
such reads make no new survivor-trigger tests. This reduction must itself be
proved before the affected lower claims are restored to full coverage.
